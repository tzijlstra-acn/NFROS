## Why Trust comes before Value

The authority slide (9) asks how the boundary is enforced. The Trust slide (10) answers that question directly. The Value slide (11) then explains what the controlled product should improve, so value is discussed only once control has been shown.

## Why slides 3 and 4 stay separate

Slide 3 shows where capacity is consumed. Slide 4 shows the consequences of that fragmentation for decisions and continuity. Merging them would lose the cause and effect.
